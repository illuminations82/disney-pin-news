From Reddit

  • First impressions of the Pink a la Mode pin boards - r/DisneyPins collectors are split on the new boards. They are shaped like oversized backer cards, complete with a retail-style hang hole at the top. One buyer likes the look but says the material feels soft and less secure than GPP boards or plain cork, and wonders how it will hold up to pins going in and out. Source
