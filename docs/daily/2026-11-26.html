Heads up

  • Quality problems on genuine pins make fakes harder to spot - a collector showed the backs of two pins they bought directly from Disney: the Mickey-head waffle pattern looks so uneven that they would have taken the pins for fakes if they hadn't bought them themselves. Another commenter said a pin from a Hidden Mickey pack bought in the parks earlier this year had a large dent and chip. So when you check a trade, a rough backstamp alone doesn't prove a pin is a scrapper. Source
