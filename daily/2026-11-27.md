## New releases

- ![Disneyland December 2026 pin one-sheet](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0801/2103/6985/files/Q1_27_December_DLR_One_Sheet_1024x1024.jpg?v=1795752803) **December 1 (Tuesday) - Beauty and the Beast 35th anniversary, both resorts** - an LR pin ($21.99), two LE 4,000 pin-on-pin designs ($24.99 each), an LE 2,000 jumbo ($84.99) and a 2-pin mystery box (LR, $34.99), plus the December Kingdom Hearts pin of the month (12 of 12, LE 4,000, $24.99). Disneyland adds two LE 3,000 mini jumbos, Buena Vista Street and Tomorrowland ($49.99 each). Disneyland says every pin on the December 1 list will be sold through a virtual queue at Disney Pin Traders. [Source](https://disneypinsblog.com/blogs/news/december-2026-disney-parks-pin-preview)
- ![Disneyland December 2026 pin one-sheet](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0801/2103/6985/files/Q1_27_December_DLR_One_Sheet_1024x1024.jpg?v=1795752803) **December 2 (Wednesday) - Candlelight Processional, Disneyland only** - LE 3,000, $24.99, pin-on-pin with glitter. [Source](https://disneypinsblog.com/blogs/news/december-2026-disney-parks-pin-preview)
- ![Walt Disney World December 2026 pin one-sheet](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0801/2103/6985/files/Q1_27_December_WDW_One_Sheet_1024x1024.jpg?v=1795752950) **December 8 (Tuesday) - Rogue One 10th, Bedknobs and Broomsticks 55th, Winter 2026** - all LR at $21.99 at both resorts. Pins of the month (12 of 12): Walt's Magical Moments at Disneyland (LE 2,500, $24.99) and Windows of Attraction at Walt Disney World (LE 2,500, $26.99). [Source](https://disneypinsblog.com/blogs/news/december-2026-disney-parks-pin-preview)
- ![Disneyland December 2026 pin one-sheet](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0801/2103/6985/files/Q1_27_December_DLR_One_Sheet_1024x1024.jpg?v=1795752803) **December 15 (Tuesday) - winter couples series and jumbos** - Meeko and Pocahontas, Sven and Kristoff, Bruno and Cinderella, and Lady and Darling (LE 4,000, $26.99 each), a Merry Christmas 2026 pin (LR, $21.99), a snowman 2-pin mystery box (LR, $34.99) and the Magical Theater pin of the month (12 of 12, LE 4,500, $24.99) at both resorts. The LE 2,000 jumbo ($79.99) is Figaro at Disneyland and Pluto at Walt Disney World. [Source](https://disneypinsblog.com/blogs/news/december-2026-disney-parks-pin-preview)
- ![Walt Disney World December 2026 pin one-sheet](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0801/2103/6985/files/Q1_27_December_WDW_One_Sheet_1024x1024.jpg?v=1795752950) **December 22 (Tuesday) - Lunar New Year 2027 and Happy New Year 2027** - a Lunar New Year pin in LE 2,000 ($24.99), an LR Lunar New Year Mickey and Minnie pin ($21.99) and an LE 3,000 Happy New Year 2027 pin ($24.99) at both resorts. Disneyland also gets the Play Along Pins pin of the month (12 of 12, LE 2,000, $24.99) and Disneyland Is Your Land (6 of 6, LE 3,000, $24.99). Walt Disney World gets the Enchanted Doors pin of the month (12 of 12, LE 2,500, $26.99). Disney notes that artwork, dates and edition sizes can still change. [Source](https://disneypinsblog.com/blogs/news/december-2026-disney-parks-pin-preview)
